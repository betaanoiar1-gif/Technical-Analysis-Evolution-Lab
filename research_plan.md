# Research Plan

## Phase 1 — Baseline

Run buy-and-hold and simple non-optimized technical strategies on multiple assets/timeframes. Record costs, exposure, drawdown, and number of trades.

## Phase 2 — School exploration

Search trend, momentum, volatility, breakout, volume, and price-action families. Each hypothesis must be falsifiable and must survive development constraints before it can reach the holdout.

## Phase 3 — Evolution

Use validation evidence to select elites, mutate parameters, and shift the next candidate distribution. Failures are retained as evidence; the system does not simply keep the highest historical return.

## Phase 4 — Robustness

Test sensitivity to fees, slippage, small price perturbations, sub-periods, and walk-forward windows. Reject fragile candidates.

## Phase 5 — Independent holdout

Only the final accepted development candidate is evaluated on the untouched holdout. Holdout results are for measurement, never for tuning.

## Phase 6 — Later releases

Future releases may add multi-asset portfolios, short/futures research, richer price-action pattern grammars, statistical significance corrections, model-assisted hypothesis generation, and Pine Script export. Live execution is deliberately excluded until the research system earns that privilege through independent evidence.
